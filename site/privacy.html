Habits: Privacy policy

Effective 8 November 2026

The short version: Habits keeps everything on your phone. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. The developer never receives your data.

Who this is from

Habits is a personal habit tracker for iPhone, made by akram_elfadili (“the developer”). The way to reach the developer is the GitHub issues page of the project.

What is stored on your phone

Only what you enter or choose, plus a small log of the app’s own errors:

  • Your habits: names, categories, schedules and targets.
  • Your daily logs, frozen days and any notes you write.
  • Reminder times and days.
  • Settings: appearance, the evening review and its time, and whether reminders hide habit names.
  • A local diagnostics log of the last few hundred app events. It holds technical facts such as event names, timestamps and internal numbers. It never holds habit names or notes.

Where it goes

Nowhere. The data lives only in the app on your phone, and in your own device backup (iCloud or a computer backup) if you have turned that on in iOS. The app makes no network requests and contains no third-party code that sends data anywhere. Because the developer holds no copy, there is nothing the developer could share, sell or lose.

Notifications and the lock screen

Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your phone. They are not sent from a server.

Some habits are personal, so by default a reminder reads “Habit reminder” and does not show the habit’s name. You can turn on names in Settings › Notifications › Hide habit names. If you do, the name appears in the notification title and can be seen on the lock screen. iOS can also hide the text of all notifications until you unlock the phone: Settings › Notifications › Show Previews.

Export and diagnostics

Export data creates a JSON or CSV file of your habits and logs. You choose whether notes are included. The file goes only where you send it from the iOS share sheet. The app deletes its temporary copy when the share sheet closes.

Copy diagnostics puts a short text on your clipboard: app version, phone model, iOS version, language, time zone, and recent technical events. It contains no habit names or notes. Check it before you paste it anywhere public.

Your rights

  • Access and portability: Settings › Export data gives you everything in a standard format.
  • Correction and erasure: edit or delete any habit and its history inside the app, or delete the app to remove all of its data from your phone.
  • The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to request from the developer. These rights are met by the app itself.

Children

Habits is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect anything from anyone.

Changes

If the app ever changes how it handles data, this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change. The version history of this page is public in the project’s repository.

Contact

Questions about privacy: open an issue on the GitHub issues page. Please do not put personal information in a public issue.
